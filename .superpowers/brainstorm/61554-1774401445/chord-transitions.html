Key Concepts

Results ranked by total effort. Combines fret distance + number of pedal/lever changes + common tones retained. Least effort first.
Clicking a result highlights both voicings on the fretboard. Chord A in cyan, chord B in red — so you can see the physical relationship on the neck.
