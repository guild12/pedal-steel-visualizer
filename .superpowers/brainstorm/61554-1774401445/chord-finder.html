Fretboard Highlighting — Search Results

After selecting G#7, the fretboard lights up showing every fret where you can play it. Intensity/style shows how complete the voicing is.

Finding G#7 — Fretboard highlights

Key Design Decisions

Highlight intensity = voicing quality. Bright solid border = complete chord, no pedals needed. Dimmer dashed border = partial or requires pedals. Lets you scan the fretboard and instantly see your best options.
Only chord tones light up. Non-chord-tone strings at that fret stay dim/hidden. You see exactly which strings to pick.
Pedal badges below the fret show what controls you'd need to engage. Click the fret to see full details in the popover.
